## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am a **PhD researcher at KTH Royal Institute of Technology** working at the intersection of nuclear engineering, plasma physics, and computational simulation. My research focuses on **runaway electron–induced damage of plasma-facing components** in fusion reactors — developing coupled multiphysics workflows to predict how materials survive under extreme conditions.

I hold an **MSc in Nuclear Engineering** (*cum laude*) and a **BSc in Physical Engineering** (*cum laude*), both from **Politecnico di Milano**.

I combine deep physics understanding with hands-on software engineering, HPC deployment, and end-to-end ownership of complex simulation pipelines — from geometry and meshing through execution, analysis, and scientific communication.
